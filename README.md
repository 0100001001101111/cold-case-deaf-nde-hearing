# COLD CASE NDE-03 — What deaf ears hear when the heart stops

Family: nde  |  Disposition: **POSTABLE_AS_COLD_CASE**  |  claim_authority=NONE
Members: born-deaf-archive-v1, F3

## The case
People deaf from birth reporting sound during near-death experiences would be one of the strangest facts in the field; one-sided-deaf people hearing on their good side only would be one of the most deflationary. The archives contain exactly two born-deaf reporters, nine one-sided-deaf narrators - and a pattern nobody predicted: the archive's dominant story is hearing RESTORED, explicitly including the deaf side.

## How it died
Base rates: 37 self-identified deaf rows produced an expected bundle count of 0.13, so zero observed is uninformative. The machine coder for all 4,053 hearing-comparison answers failed its blind precision floor on two load-bearing categories (0.60 and 0.70 against 0.80) and only 16 impaired reporters landed codes against a floor of 30 - capped at DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY. On boundary-transition sounds in deaf reporters the archives are simply silent (0 cases).

## What still stands
Two verified born-deaf reporters with codable answers; 8 of 9 one-sided-deaf narrators describing restored or both-sides hearing; one hearing person's unilateral transition buzz proving one-sidedness occurs. Ceiling: case notes, not statistics.

## What would revive it
Deaf experiencers. Any archive, clinic, community, or interpreter network reaching born-deaf or single-sided-deaf people who report NDEs or sleep-boundary experiences - a few dozen structured interviews would out-power everything on file.

## Card fields (G18 contract)
- plain_meaning: The question of what deaf people experience at the edge of death is nearly untouched: existing archives hold a handful of cases and they say something odd - restored hearing - that no theory ordered.
- what_it_is_not: Not evidence of anything paranormal, because expectation and narrative convention can produce restoration stories; and not a null, because the sample is a rounding error - the question has never been powered.
- next_decisive_test: A preregistered interview protocol (already drafted into the lab's prospective program) asking one-sided-deaf experiencers separately about boundary sounds and deep-experience hearing.
- what_we_need: Contact with deaf and single-sided-deaf communities and clinicians (audiology, CI clinics) willing to relay a short structured questionnaire to members who have had NDEs or intense sleep-boundary episodes.
